General Instructions

  1. Total duration of the examination is 180 Minutes (3 Hours).
  2. The clock in the top right corner displays the remaining time left for the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end automatically.
  3. The paper consists of 65 Questions with a total of 100 Marks divided into two sections:
    • Section 1 (General Aptitude): Questions 1 to 5 (1 Mark each) and Questions 6 to 10 (2 Marks each) = 15 Marks.
    • Section 2 (Computer Science & Engineering Mathematics): Questions 11 to 35 (1 Mark each) and Questions 36 to 65 (2 Marks each) = 85 Marks.

Question Types & Marking Scheme

GATE 2027 Examination Instructions

1. Duration: 180 Minutes. Automatic submission upon timer expiry.

2. Navigation: Click any question number on the right palette or use Save & Next / Previous buttons.

3. Saving Answers: To save your answer, click Save & Next. Answering and clicking Mark for Review & Next will also be considered for evaluation in GATE!

4. Negative Marking:

  • MCQ 1 Mark: −0.33 deduction for incorrect response
  • MCQ 2 Mark: −0.67 deduction for incorrect response
  • MSQ (1 & 2 Mark): NO negative marking. Must select all correct options with no wrong options to receive marks.
  • NAT (1 & 2 Mark): NO negative marking. Enter numerical answer within precision.

5. Scientific Calculator: Click the "Calculator" button in the top bar to open the virtual calculator.
